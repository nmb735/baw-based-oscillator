Measured reflection coefficients $S_{11}$-$S_{44}$ of the 4-port subarray (left axis, installed with cables, cable bank not de-embedded) and forward read range of the Alien Higgs-3 tag (right axis), over 0.80-0.94 GHz. The two are independent measurements: the reflections were acquired on the NanoVNA, the read range on a Voyantic Tagformance Pro against its own reader antenna, not against this subarray. Traces are Savitzky-Golay smoothed. The inset magnifies 863.0-870.0 MHz, the slice marked by the dashed rules. The shaded vertical band indicates the European UHF RFID band (ETSI, 865.7-867.5 MHz).
